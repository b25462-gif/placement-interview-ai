BEHAVIORAL & HR INTERVIEW QUESTIONS — PLACEMENT PREPARATION
============================================================

## BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS (Use STAR Method: Situation, Task, Action, Result)

ABOUT YOURSELF:
1. Tell me about yourself. (Keep it 2 minutes, professional)
2. What are your biggest strengths? Give examples.
3. What is your biggest weakness? How are you working on it?
4. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
5. Why did you choose Computer Science?

TEAMWORK & LEADERSHIP:
1. Tell me about a time you worked in a difficult team.
2. Describe a situation where you led a team to success.
3. Have you ever had a conflict with a teammate? How did you resolve it?
4. Tell me about a time you mentored someone junior to you.
5. Describe a time when you had to collaborate across departments.

PROBLEM SOLVING:
1. Tell me about the most challenging technical problem you solved.
2. Describe a project that failed. What did you learn?
3. Give an example of a time you had to think outside the box.
4. Tell me about a time you had to learn something very quickly.
5. Describe a situation where you had to make a decision with limited information.

WORK ETHIC & PRESSURE:
1. How do you handle multiple deadlines at the same time?
2. Tell me about a time you worked under extreme pressure.
3. Describe a time when you went above and beyond for a project.
4. How do you prioritize tasks when everything seems urgent?
5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager/professor.

## HR QUESTIONS

MOTIVATION:
1. Why do you want to work at our company?
2. Why should we hire you over other candidates?
3. What motivates you to come to work every day?
4. What kind of work environment do you thrive in?
5. What are you passionate about outside of work?

SALARY & GROWTH:
1. What are your salary expectations?
2. Are you willing to relocate for this job?
3. How soon can you join if selected?
4. Do you have any competing offers?
5. What does success look like to you in this role?

COMPANY-SPECIFIC:
1. What do you know about our company?
2. Which of our products/services interests you the most and why?
3. How do you stay updated with the latest technology trends?
4. What unique value would you bring to our team?

SITUATIONAL QUESTIONS:
1. If you were given a project with unclear requirements, what would you do?
2. How would you handle a situation where you made a mistake in production?
3. If a teammate is not contributing to the project, how would you handle it?
4. What would you do if you strongly disagreed with your team's technical approach?
5. How would you explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical stakeholder?

## TIPS FOR BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEWS

STAR Method:
- Situation: Set the context briefly
- Task: What was your responsibility?
- Action: What specific steps did YOU take?
- Result: What was the measurable outcome?

Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- Giving vague or generic answers
- Not quantifying results (use numbers, %)
- Speaking negatively about past employers
- Not preparing questions to ask the interviewer
- Rambling without a clear structure

Power Phrases:
- "I took ownership of..."
- "The impact was..."
- "I collaborated with..."
- "I improved X by Y%..."
- "I learned that..."
