JOB TITLE: Software Engineer — New Grad (L3)
COMPANY: Google India
LOCATION: Bangalore / Hyderabad
TYPE: Full-Time | Campus Placement

ABOUT THE ROLE
---------------
We're looking for passionate software engineers who love building scalable, efficient systems.
As an L3 engineer at Google, you will design, develop, and maintain Google's products and infrastructure.

RESPONSIBILITIES
-----------------
- Design and implement large-scale distributed systems
- Write clean, well-tested, and efficient code in Python, Java, C++ or Go
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on product features
- Participate in code reviews and technical design discussions
- Debug production issues and improve system reliability
- Contribute to Google's open-source projects

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS
-----------------------
- Bachelor's/Master's in Computer Science or related field
- Strong foundation in Data Structures and Algorithms
- Proficiency in at least one of: Python, Java, C++, Go
- Understanding of object-oriented design and software engineering principles
- Experience with databases (SQL/NoSQL)

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
-------------------------
- Experience with distributed systems, cloud platforms (GCP, AWS)
- Knowledge of machine learning and AI/ML frameworks
- Open-source contributions
- Competitive programming achievements (ICPC, CodeForces, LeetCode)
- Prior internship experience at a tech company
- Knowledge of system design principles

TECHNICAL SKILLS EXPECTED
--------------------------
Core CS: Data Structures, Algorithms, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, DBMS
Programming: Python, Java, C++, Go, JavaScript
Systems: Distributed Systems, Microservices, REST APIs
Cloud: GCP, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
Databases: BigQuery, Spanner, MySQL, MongoDB

INTERVIEW PROCESS
------------------
Round 1: Online Assessment (DSA + Aptitude) — 90 minutes
Round 2: Technical Interview 1 (Data Structures & Algorithms)
Round 3: Technical Interview 2 (System Design)
Round 4: Behavioral / Googleyness Interview
Round 5: HR & Offer Discussion

PACKAGE
--------

Base: ₹20-35 LPA
Stock (RSU): ₹8-15 LPA (4-year vest)
Bonus: 15-20% of base

CULTURE
--------
Google values: Googleyness, innovation, collaboration, and passion for technology.
We believe in psychological safety, diversity, and continuous learning.
